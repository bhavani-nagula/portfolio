About Me

Building the infrastructure
the cloud runs on

I'm Bhavani — a Cloud & DevOps Engineer based in Telangana, India, who graduated in Electronics & Communication Engineering from Malla Reddy College of Engineering in 2024. My journey into the cloud started with curiosity about how modern software gets built, deployed, and kept running at scale.

I specialise in designing CI/CD pipelines on Azure DevOps, provisioning infrastructure-as-code with Terraform, and containerising workloads with Docker & Kubernetes (AKS). I embed security early in the pipeline using SonarQube for code quality gates and Trivy for container image scanning — a practice that separates good DevOps from great DevSecOps.

My goal is to join a team solving hard infrastructure problems at companies like Amazon, Microsoft, or Google — contributing to platforms that millions of people rely on.
